Energy Transition

Solarizing Cold Chain Infrastructure

Industrial freezing and refrigerated warehousing are highly energy-intensive. Standard operations rely on diesel grids, adding carbon loads to delicate island microclimates. Wish Holdings is systematically replacing conventional energy sources with localized solar arrays.

With **1.2 Megawatts of installed solar capacity** across our docks and freezing hubs, we satisfy up to 85% of our daily power requirements. We are working toward a 100% carbon-neutral processing cycle by 2028.

Circular Flow

Zero Waste Initiative

To maximize raw resource utility, we operate a circular zero-waste workflow. No organic tuna material is discarded during filleting.

Tuna trim, organs, and bones are channeled to our agricultural processing unit, where they are converted into high-grade organic fish meal (for aquaculture feeds) and liquid bio-fertilizers.

This system prevents marine dumping, preserves harbor hygiene, and provides valuable organic nutrients to local agricultural co-ops on adjacent islands.

Circular Target achieved

Currently, 98.4% of all raw fish weight offloaded is successfully converted into export seafood or agricultural value-added products.
