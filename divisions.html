Division 01

Sustainable Seafood & Industrial Exports

The core commercial driver of Wish Holdings is our integrated seafood sourcing, processing, and export operation. We collect high-grade skipjack and yellowfin tuna caught individually by Maldivian fishing crews using zero-net pole-and-line techniques.

This premium catch is brought within hours to our processing facilities, where it undergoes inspection, temperature audits, and cut classification under rigorous food safety certificates (HACCP, FSSC 22000). We export chilled loins, super-frozen blocks, and our retail canned lines.

Division 02

Wish Harbour Infrastructure & Logistics

Our infrastructure division drives our regional footprint growth. Through the **Wish Harbour Project**, we design and fund marine logistics networks, automated cold-storage warehouses, and processing hubs on selected Maldivian islands (Himmafushi, Kandholhudhoo, and Kanandhoo).

A unique aspect of our projects is the integration of light industrial seafood hubs with eco-tourism. This includes building processing docks alongside luxury tourist accommodations and marine conservation research laboratories.

Division 03

Renewable Energy & Marine CSR

Sustainable action is integrated directly into our holding company structure rather than treated as an afterthought. Our Renewable Energy division builds and runs high-performance photovoltaic microgrids that supply power to our facilities and fleet operations.

Through our marine corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs, we actively fund coral restoration networks, marine waste cleanup initiatives, and educational scholarships for children of local artisanal fishermen cooperatives.
