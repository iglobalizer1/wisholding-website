Himmafushi Hub

Modern Processing Lines

Our primary production operations are centered at the Himmafushi Processing Plant. Designed to meet strict European Union and US FDA sanitation policies, the plant manages a continuous cold chain from dock reception to container packing.

With a capacity of **15,000 Metric Tons annually**, the facility features automated loin cutting machines, IQF (Individually Quick Frozen) lines, and a dedicated canning hall. We operate under a "no temperature breach" system, ensuring all raw seafood is maintained below -2°C prior to freezing.

The entire facility operates on an integrated rooftop solar farm and redirects organic processing trim into fishmeal and fertilizer production, maintaining a zero-waste policy.

Industrial Workflow

Our Processing Workflow

Ensuring complete product integrity at each operational stage.

01

Vessel Offloading & Grading

Artisanal vessels dock at our solar-powered berths. Every single fish is inspected for temperature, graded for fat content, tagged with a unique barcode indicating vessel origin, and logged in our digital database.

02

Primary Gutting & Chilling

Tuna is washed, headed, and gutted in temperature-controlled rooms kept below 10°C. Raw meat is graded for color index and sent to blast chillers.

03

Loin Processing & Canning

Premium cuts are filleted into loins, vacuum packed, and frozen. Trim cuts are sent to the canned tuna section for packing in premium olive oil or organic brine.

04

Super-Freezing & Storage

Finished loins are frozen to -60°C to lock in sashimi quality and color. Cans are sterilized in steam autoclaves, labeled, and boxed for container transport.

Laboratory Audits

Rigorous Food Safety Assurance

Our on-site testing facility is equipped to perform daily chemical and microbiological testing. We test each batch for histamine levels, heavy metal presence, bacterial count, and organoleptic properties.

Histamine Control Program

We enforce a strict thermal limit program that limits histamine levels below 10ppm, well exceeding the EU and US FDA safety requirement of 50ppm.

No container is released for export without a signed certificate of quality analysis issued directly by our Lead Biochemist, ensuring full safety for global consumers.
