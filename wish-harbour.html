WISH HARBOUR

Three Islands. One Ocean Economy.

Wish Harbour represents the core vision of Wish Holdings Pvt Ltd — transforming the Maldives into the Indian Ocean's global hub for sustainable fisheries, eco-tourism, and ethical innovation.

The Wish Harbour brand encompasses three strategic islands, each designed to strengthen a different pillar of the company's mission: ocean-based production, community-driven sustainability, and eco-conscious tourism.

Together, they form the living embodiment of our vision — balancing business growth with marine stewardship and social impact.

WISH HARBOUR HIMMAFUSHI

The Heart of Ocean Industry & Innovation

Main Focus: Advanced Tuna Processing & Value-Added Production

Wish Harbour Himmafushi stands as the industrial cornerstone of Wish Holdings — a state-of-the-art complex for tuna and seafood production, ensuring premium Maldivian products reach the world with integrity and freshness.

Key Facilities:

Fish Purchase & Processing Plant – For freshly caught tuna and seafood.
Cold Storage Plant – Ensuring uninterrupted preservation and export quality.
Canned Tuna Unit – A modern cannery for global-scale distribution.
Sausage Plant – Producing high-quality fish sausages for local and export markets.
Fish Paste Plant – Specialized in Maldivian-style fish paste for regional trade.
Ice Plant – Supporting the fisheries supply chain and storage logistics.
Essence: Himmafushi powers the economic engine of Wish Holdings — a hub of innovation, efficiency, and sustainable industrial development rooted in the Maldivian sea.

WISH HARBOUR KANDHOLHUDHOO

The Soul of Marine Conservation & Ocean Experience

Main Focus: Eco-Tourism, Marine Research & Community Engagement

Wish Harbour Kandholhudhoo bridges the world of conservation and experience, offering both environmental learning and sustainable living through reef-based tourism and educational interaction.

Key Developments:

Reef Fish Purchase & Processing Plant – Supporting reef-friendly fishing practices.
Fresh & Frozen Tuna Cold Storage Facility – Maintaining freshness for local and international markets.
Ice Plant – Ensuring the sustainability of reef fish operations.
30 Eco Villas – Oceanfront villas designed for eco-conscious visitors.
Marine Conservation Hub Base – A dedicated center for coral restoration, marine studies, and sustainability training.
Guided Fish & Reef Ecology Tours – Hands-on experiences connecting visitors to marine biodiversity.
Interpretive Center & Exhibits – Showcasing Maldivian ocean heritage and marine research.
Community & Cultural Engagement Programs – Empowering local communities through education and eco-tourism.
Essence: Kandholhudhoo represents the heart of sustainability — merging environmental protection with cultural pride and experiential tourism.

WISH HARBOUR KANANDHOO

The Spirit of Sustainable Development

Main Focus: Sustainable Tuna Processing & Responsible Tourism

Wish Harbour Kanandhoo combines production excellence with environmental guardianship — ensuring that every catch and every stay reflects Maldivian responsibility toward the sea.

Key Developments:

Fish Purchase & Processing Plant – Focused on fresh and frozen tuna export.
Cold Storage Plant – Supporting supply chain reliability.
Ice Plant – Serving fisheries and local needs.
50 Villas – Designed for luxury eco-tourism with ocean-view experiences.
Sustainable Fishing & Local Integration Programs – Partnering with traditional fishermen to maintain ecological balance.
Strict Environmental Protocols – Ensuring zero waste, responsible waste management, and carbon neutrality.
Essence: Kanandhoo embodies balanced progress — integrating luxury, livelihood, and long-term environmental stewardship.
