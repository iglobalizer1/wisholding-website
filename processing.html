Plant Overview

WISH CORALIA is the premier processing arm of Wish Holdings, situated strategically at Himmafushi. The plant is engineered to support Maldivian artisanal fishermen while maintaining an unbroken cold chain for high-grade international seafood distribution.

Main Objectives

  • Guarantee fair purchasing prices for local pole-and-line fishermen.
  • Maintain EU and FDA export-grade temperature-controlled hygiene standards.
  • Maximize yield efficiency through state-of-the-art tuna loin and chunk preparation.
  • Provide 100% catch-to-package digital barcode traceability.

Product Processing Flow

01

Docking & QC

Fresh tuna received directly from vessels and core temperature tested.

02

Grading & Loining

Precision hand-loining into Akami and Otoro cuts under hygienic conditions.

03

Blast Freezing

Ultra-low temperature blast freezing preserves cellular structure and color.

04

Export Packing

Vacuum packing, master carton sealing, and cold-chain container loading.

Product Range

Currently producing fresh and frozen Yellowfin Tuna loins, chunks, steaks, Saku blocks, H&G, G&G, and whole round frozen Skipjack Tuna.

Quality & Compliance

Certified under HACCP, ISO 22000, FDA, and EU export approvals with continuous MFDA regulatory audit compliance.

Community & Environmental Impact

Directly supporting local fisherman livelihoods, operating solar-assisted energy systems, and committing to zero-waste marine utilization.
