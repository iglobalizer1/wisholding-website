Primary Cuts

Understanding Yellowfin Tuna Cuts

Yellowfin tuna is processed into four loins, two top (akami) loins and two bottom (otoro) loins, each type is distinct and used for specific dishes.

Top Loin

Akami

Akami, or top loin, has leaner meat with a slightly more intense flavour. It's ideal for sashimi, carpaccio, gourmet, and grilling.

Bottom Loin

Otoro

Otoro is the fattiest part of a tuna, especially near the belly. It has a sophisticated taste and distinctive fat marbling.

Front Portion

Head Chunk

The portion closest to the head of the fish. It has less size variances at the ends, and provides better yield compared to the tail loin.

Middle Portion

Centre Cut

The middle part of loin, and has uniform thickness on both ends. It has the highest yield and is the most costly part of the loin.

Tail Portion

End Chunk

The portion of the loin that is closest to the tail of the fish. It has the most size variance on both ends, and is the least costly part of the loin.

Buyer FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our standard MOQ for air-freight fresh tuna loins starts at 500 kg. For sea-freight frozen containers (20ft / 40ft reefer), MOQ is 10 metric tons, customizable based on product format.
What is the typical lead time?
Air export shipments are dispatched within 24–48 hours of harvest. Frozen sea-freight container orders typically take 7–14 business days from order confirmation to departure.
Is private labeling available?
Yes, we offer custom private labeling, customized vacuum pack printing, master carton branding, and tailored weight specifications for wholesale distributors.
What certifications do you have?
Our processing operations hold HACCP, ISO 22000, EU Export Approval, FDA Registration, and MSC Chain of Custody certification.
What shipping options are available?
We offer FOB Malé, CIF major international ports, and door-to-door temperature-monitored air/sea logistics through global freight partners.
