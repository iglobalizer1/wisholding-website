What Scales Next

The Future Vision expands our ecosystem across three harbour islands, value-added brands, and eco-tourism experiences.

  • Islands & Plants: Kandholhudhoo and Kanandhoo processing plants, reef fish operations, and expanded cold chain infrastructure.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Wish Holdings a high-value investment opportunity?
Wish Holdings integrates high-demand sustainable tuna harvesting with company-owned island infrastructure, cryogenic cold chain networks, and eco-tourism revenue diversification in one of the world's most pristine marine ecosystems.
What are the company's primary revenue streams?
Primary revenue streams include raw tuna export sales (Yellowfin & Skipjack), value-added retail consumer lines (AQUATUNA™, AZURISH™), cold storage logistics services, and eco-luxury villa hospitality.
What certifications and regulatory standards are maintained?
HACCP, ISO 22000, EU Export Approval, US FDA Registration, and MSC Chain of Custody compliance.
What is the investment engagement and diligence process?
Prospective investors submit an inquiry form or schedule an initial consultation, followed by execution of an NDA, virtual data room access, due diligence reviews, and tailored term sheet structuring.
