Future Vision

What Scales Next

The Future Vision expands our ecosystem across three harbour islands, value-added brands, and eco-tourism experiences.

  • Islands & Plants: Kandholhudhoo and Kanandhoo processing plants, reef fish operations, and expanded cold chain infrastructure.
  • Value-Added Brands: AQUATUNA™, AZURISH™, CORALPURE™ product lines, and GLACIALA™ ice systems roll-out.
  • Tourism: AZUREÍN™ and PELAGORA™ villas, Catch of Wish restaurant, and eco-tourism experiences.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Wish Holdings a good investment opportunity?
Wish Holdings integrates high-demand sustainable tuna harvesting with company-owned island infrastructure and eco-tourism revenue diversification.
What are the main revenue streams?
Primary revenue streams include raw tuna export sales, value-added products (canned, sausages, pastes), cold storage logistics services, and eco-luxury villa tourism.
What certifications and standards does Wish Holdings maintain?
HACCP, ISO 22000, EU Export Approval, FDA Registration, and MSC Chain of Custody compliance.
What is the investment timeline and process?
Interested investors submit an inquiry form or schedule an initial consultation, followed by execution of an NDA, data room access, due diligence, and tailored partnership agreement structuring.
