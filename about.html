About Us

Our Story

Wish Holdings Pvt Ltd is the flagship enterprise of the Wish Group, rooted in the heart of the Maldives — a nation defined by its ocean, its people, and its promise.

We are driven by a single purpose: to transform the Maldives into the Indian Ocean's global hub for sustainable tuna, premium seafood, eco-tourism, and ethical investment.

Our company unites traditional Maldivian fishing heritage with modern, eco-conscious innovation, developing infrastructure and operations that empower communities, protect marine life, and deliver world-class quality to global markets.

Our Purpose

Our Vision

To establish the Maldives as a leader in sustainable, ocean-driven innovation, where the nation's rich marine bounty becomes the foundation for ethical prosperity, global recognition, and environmental harmony.

We envision a future where Maldivian tuna is not just a product — but a symbol of purity, responsibility, and national pride.
